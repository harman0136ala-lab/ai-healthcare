AI in Healthcare – Overview

Artificial intelligence is reshaping global medicine by improving diagnostics, optimizing treatment, boosting hospital efficiency, and supporting clinicians. This website shows a simplified version of the research.

AI in Diagnostics

AI improves image analysis, early disease detection, and screening accuracy:

  • AI increased mammogram cancer detection.
  • Lung-cancer risk predicted earlier.
  • Retinopathy system sensitivity increased.
  • Colonoscopy adenoma detection improved to 44.7%.

    AI in Treatment & Surgery

    AI improves surgery precision and treatment efficiency:

    • Robotic surgery reduces complications.
    • AI recovery monitoring cuts readmissions.
    • AI scheduling helps in treatment hours.
    • AI radiotherapy prioritization reduces mortality rates.

    AI Challenges

    • AI still makes mistakes in diagnostics.
    • Data privacy and security risks.
    • Fear of losing human connection.
    • Bias in datasets affects fairness.

    Conclusion

    AI in healthcare is helping how doctors diagnose diseases, do treatments, and monitor patients. From analysis and early detection to safer surgeries and better follow-up care, AI supports healthcare workers rather than replacing them.

    With proper rules, training, and patient-centered design, AI can help create a healthcare system that is more accurate, efficient, and fair for everyone to use.
